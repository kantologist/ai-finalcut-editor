About the project

A private cutting room on your Mac

Most AI video tools upload your footage to a cloud editor. This one stays on your machine. You add media, pick a frame (9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, or custom), choose a model, and create. The app builds proxy frames, scores shots, plans an Edit Decision List, then writes FCPXML that Final Cut can actually import.

  • Native desktop app Native window around a local studio UI — Create, Media, Projects, Settings.
  • Your settings, not ours Aspect ratio, pacing, style, AI model, and API key live in Settings.
  • Safe EDL The model may only pick from ranked candidates. Unknown filenames are dropped.
  • Deterministic FCPXML Portrait fills, landscape fits, vertical-first grouping, slightly longer holds.

Pipeline

From folder to timeline

  1. 01 Inspect the album and index every clip
  2. 02 Sample proxy frames for vision
  3. 03 Analyze scenes and rank candidate shots
  4. 04 Plan a validated EDL with the model you chose
  5. 05 Export FCPXML for Final Cut Pro

Failed jobs can retry from the last completed step instead of starting over. Revise with a sentence: “longer holds, end on the ocean.”
