What it is

An assistant in the cutting room — not a replacement for Final Cut.

You already know how you want a piece to feel. What you don’t want is an afternoon of logging every clip. AI Final Cut watches the album, finds the strong beats, and lays a first cut you can import straight into Final Cut Pro.

Vertical for social. Widescreen for a film. Square for a feed. You choose the frame, the length, and the mood. Then you finish the work where you always finish it — on the timeline.

How you use it

  1. 01
    Drop in the shoot

    Photos and video from the card, the phone, the drone — all on your Mac.

  2. 02
    Set the picture

    Pick 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, or a custom size. Choose how long the piece should run, and a style.

  3. 03
    Open it in Final Cut

    Download the project file and import it. The cut is a starting point — yours to refine.

Stays on your Mac

Your media never leaves the machine. The app works locally. You add your own OpenAI key in Settings when you’re ready to create.

Made for the frame you chose

Clips keep their original framing and color. You choose 9:16, 16:9, or square. Vertical coverage leads; landscape follows. Holds are unhurried.

Revise in plain language

“Longer holds. More people. End on the ocean.” A new version is saved. You keep directing; it keeps cutting.

You keep the last word

Nothing is locked. Change pacing, swap shots, grade — all in Final Cut, the way you already work.
